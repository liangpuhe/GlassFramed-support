# GlassFramed support

GlassFramed is a Mac menu bar app that sets a public-domain masterpiece from a great museum collection as your desktop wallpaper each day, with a short introduction to the work.

- **Privacy policy:** [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). GlassFramed collects no data.
- **Help or bug reports:** [open an issue](https://github.com/liangpuhe/GlassFramed-support/issues/new) or email puheliang@gmail.com.

## Frequently asked questions

**The wallpaper didn't change on one of my Spaces.**
macOS keeps a separate desktop picture for each Space. GlassFramed updates a Space when you switch to it.

**Where do the artworks come from?**
From the open-access collections of The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art, the Rijksmuseum, the J. Paul Getty Museum and, if you add a free API key, the Smithsonian. You can turn each one on or off in Settings → Museums.

**What does "AI-generated" mean on a description?**
When neither the museum nor Wikipedia has a description, GlassFramed can write a short one with Apple Intelligence, entirely on your Mac. These descriptions are always labelled and may contain mistakes. Turn them off in Settings → Introduction.

**How do I stop GlassFramed from opening at login?**
turn off "Open GlassFramed at login" in GlassFramed Settings, or System Settings → General → Login Items.
