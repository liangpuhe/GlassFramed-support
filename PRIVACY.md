# GlassFramed Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

GlassFramed does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What GlassFramed does on your Mac

- It downloads artwork images and descriptions from the public open-access services of the museums you turn on in Settings: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Art Institute of Chicago, Cleveland Museum of Art, Rijksmuseum, J. Paul Getty Museum and the Smithsonian.
- If "Use Wikipedia when a museum has no description" is on, it fetches a short summary from Wikidata and Wikipedia.
- These requests contain only what is needed to find an artwork, such as a search query or an object ID. Like any web request, they reveal your IP address to the service you contact. Each museum's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
- If "Write a description with on-device AI" is on and your Mac supports Apple Intelligence, a short description is written by Apple's on-device model, entirely on your Mac. The artwork's catalogue details are not sent anywhere for this. AI-written text is always labelled as AI-generated.
- Images, history and settings are stored only on your Mac, inside GlassFramed's sandboxed container.

## Optional features you control

- **API key** (Smithsonian): if you add one, it is stored in your macOS Keychain and sent only to that museum.
- **Your own folder:** if you choose one, GlassFramed reads images from it (read-only) and never uploads them.
- **Notifications** are shown locally on your Mac.
